# Async task correctness audit

This inventory covers the 94 registered application targets in the production
and subnet-operator registries, including factory-built financial targets and
retired compatibility handlers. The built-in `RunPost` dispatcher is covered
separately by the task-helper tests below. Unregistered/manual utilities are not
claimed as queue targets.

Read each row as a coverage map, not a claim that a function-name reference or
successful enqueue proves the business effect. The new adapter tests execute
serialized targets, inspect committed business rows or exported values, and
inspect completion and successor rows. Existing domain suites cover deeper
financial, transport, geolocation and publication protocols.

## Shared task boundaries

- `task/claim_function_starvation_test.go`: an old producer backlog prevents
  a newer consumer from admission in the negative control; bounded fair turns
  admit it while the backlog remains.
- `task/task_claim_recheck_test.go`: transaction barriers force deletion,
  future leases and held rows between discovery and claim; unrelated work
  progresses and released rows become claimable.
- The `task` helper suites cover run-once generations, heartbeat, cancellation,
  lost acknowledgements, finalization, retained Post results and repeated Post.
- `db_connection_owner_test.go` and the payment connection guard cover reuse
  of one owned session, prohibited nesting and unknown-commit refusal.

The production closure evidence and the technique for comparing synchronous
effects with queued execution are in [monitor/DEBUG.md](../monitor/DEBUG.md).
Those bounded production runs did not deploy the worker or apply migrations.

## Validation scope

The targeted task-helper, fairness, deadline reconciliation, synchronous CLI,
grant replay, payment reconciliation and new task-adapter suites passed during
this investigation. The starvation test includes the failing admission policy
as a negative control. Separate pre-fix controls reproduced the grant,
onboarding, payment connection and stale-chain failures.

This is not a green full-repository test run. The initial root tests required a
temporary overlay for unrelated ARIN fixture compilation errors. The upstream
merge supplied that fix; root compilation and the selected transaction and
schema-guard tests now run without the overlay. Integration tests also retain
coverage of old serialized closure handoffs through the current `RunPost` path,
and every coherent migration prefix through the appended deadline guard.

The final check also retains failures in
`TestForceCloseBudgetRedisReservationAndParentCancel/owner_budget` and the two
`TestCloseExpired*KeepsTaskAndIdleCadence` accounting/quarantine cases. The first
expects escrow projections before the retained debit owner runs; the latter
assert unchanged task metadata across retry. The owner-budget and accounting
case failures were also reproduced against the committed baseline. These tests
were not disabled or relaxed. The loaded-subpage and parent-cancellation budget
cases passed the final run.
