METRIC BOUNDARIES

What these numbers do—and do not—say.

TTFB: successful positive-request samples; headline p50/p95 values are medians of nine run quantiles, not pooled quantiles.

Large-transfer rate: successful HTTP200 downloads ≥1MiB, received bytes divided by total completion time; decimal Mbps=bytes/s×8/10⁶. p95 is the fast tail, not a struggling-flow guarantee.

Aggregate rate: all delivered payload across the configured workload, not physical link capacity, sustained per-client bandwidth, or the p50 transfer statistic.

Unpublished candidate metrics: candidate TTFB and per-transfer quantiles are not in these allowed public score/proof summaries, so no candidate series is invented.

R9 is the independent sample count for Welch. Per-request N and within-run block_se are not substitutes for nine independent runs.
