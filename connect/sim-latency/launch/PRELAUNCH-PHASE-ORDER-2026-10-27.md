# Independent trusted calibration and reviewer qualification

The frozen operator-authored no-op/worse/better calibration does not execute
competition submissions and does not depend on a winner-review decision.
It may therefore precede the pending reviewer qualification. This changes
operational ordering only; it does not waive a launch requirement.

- All builds, unit tests and model calls must be terminal before timed scoring.
- While timed scoring holds the canonical slot, only code analysis/editing and
  routine low-overhead monitoring may proceed; no unrelated compilation,
  fixture execution, tests or model calls may overlap it.
- The frozen source, R9, 26.9% margin, significance, reference identities,
  one-shot/no-resampling rules and three-hour evaluation cap remain unchanged.
- The currently approved calibration still requires the fresh bridge and two
  independently drawn seeds. A requested one-seed compromise is **not approved**
  and cannot be applied without the owner's explicit response.
- All six genuine three-model review cases, source-test attestation, empirical
  calibration, exact-target host readiness, both runtime roles and fenced
  activation must pass before the competition opens. Uncertainty remains a
  hold, never approval; the failed V6 attempt is not accepted.

The trusted calibration can advance while the next reviewer-fixture correction
is analyzed, without measuring under competing build/test/model load. Actual
reference/scoring acceptance and final launch readiness remain separate claims.
